A 6-week journey of faith, community, and invitation — ending in the most powerful Sunday of the year.

6-Week Sprint · Quarterly Campaign

We believe everyone deserves a seat at the table. This season, we're asking every member to carry 3 names — friends, family, and neighbors — and trust God to move. Here's your 6-week roadmap to Friends Day.

The 6-Week Journey
BFG
Pre-Launch
Leaders prep groups
  • Text each member personally
  • Pray, bring 3 names
  • Build expectation in your group
W1
Launch Sunday
3 names. One card.
  • Announce Friends Day publicly
  • Hand out prayer cards
  • 3 names per card to pray over
  • BFG leaders collect names
  • Goal: 1,200+ names
W2
Prayer Wall
Make faith visible.
  • Build lobby prayer wall
  • Pray over every chair
  • Photo wall for social media
  • BFGs pray names weekly
W3
Mass Invite
Biggest crowd Sunday.
  • Text your person — now
  • Whole church sends together
  • Words on screen in real time
  • Announce live wall count
W4
Testimony Sunday
Real stories. Cards out.
  • 3 people · 2 min · no script — authentic testimonies from real members
  • Guest who stayed shares their BFG life change journey
  • Each card: 3 names to personally invite for Friends Day
W5
Confirmation
Stand up if they said yes.
  • Public commitment moment
  • Pray over those waiting
  • BFGs confirm each guest
  • Know your count by Friday
W6
Final Prep
You are a host.
  • Teach congregation to host
  • Commission guest team
  • Sat night vigil 8pm–8am
  • Full volunteer rehearsal
The Main Event
Friends Day

Fill the room. Transform a life.

  • Prayer team on site from 7am — covering every seat
  • Reserved guest parking — honor every first-time visitor
  • Kids Express check-in — fast, friendly, joyful
  • Preach for the undecided — a message that moves hearts
  • Clear altar invitation — receive those who say yes
  • Card + BFG invite per guest — every guest leaves connected
How to Invite

Pray 3 Names

Write down 3 people God has placed on your heart. Pray over them daily this week.

Send the Invite

Text, call, or share this email with your person. Make it personal — your story matters.

Bring Them With You

Pick them up, sit with them, and introduce them to your WHC family on Friends Day.

Follow-Up Week

Retention lives here. What happens the week after Friends Day determines whether your guest returns. Don't miss this step.

  • Text within 2 hours of the service ending
  • Card in mail by Tuesday with a personal note
  • Personal call Wednesday — just to check in
  • Invite to a BFG this week — they need community
  • Return wall cards with a handwritten note
